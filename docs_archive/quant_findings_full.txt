The most severe problems are: (1) **Look-ahead bias in backtest engine** (entry at next bar's close, not next open), (2) **No transaction costs modeled** in trade simulator, (3) **Missing Sharpe/Sortino/Calmar calculations** (all return 0.0), (4) **No purged/embargoed walk-forward CV**, (5) **No multiple testing correction** in optimization.\n\n---\n\n## 1. Backtest Methodology Correctness\n\n### CRITICAL: Look-Ahead Bias - Entry at Current Bar Close\n**File:** `engine/backtest/backtest_engine.py:56-60`\n```python\nentry_price = float(df[\"Close\"].iloc[entry_index])\n```\n**Issue:** Signals are generated on bar `i` (using data up to and including bar `i`), but entry is executed at **bar `i`'s close**. In reality, you only know the close *after* the bar completes, so execution must be at **bar `i+1` open**. This creates perfect hindsight - you're entering at a price you couldn't have known at signal generation time.\n\n### CRITICAL: Zero Transaction Costs\n**File:** `engine/backtest/trade_simulator.py:216-221`\n```python\ntransaction_cost=TransactionCost(\n    brokerage=0.0,\n    slippage=0.0,\n    taxes=0.0,\n    total=0.0,\n),\n```\n**Issue:** `TradeProcessor.process()` is called (line 101-104 in backtest_engine.py) but `TradeSimulator.simulate()` hardcodes all costs to zero. No slippage, commission, STT, GST modeled. This inflates returns significantly, especially for high-frequency strategies.\n\n### CRITICAL: No Purged/Embargoed Walk-Forward CV\n**File:** `engine/research/walk_forward_windows.py:35-36`\n```python\nvalidation_start = train_end  # NO GAP - immediate adjacency\n```\n**Issue:** Training window ends at `train_end`, validation starts **immediately at same timestamp**. No embargo/purge gap to prevent leakage from:\n- Indicator lookback periods bleeding into validation\n- Serial correlation in returns\n- Market regime continuity\n\n**Required:** Add `embargo_period` (e.g., 20-50 bars) between `train_end` and `validation_start`.\n\n### HIGH: Signal-to-Trade Conversion Uses Current Bar\n**File:** `engine/core/strategy_runner.py:64-68`\n```python\nfor i in range(start_index, len(data)):\n    window = data.iloc[: i + 1]  # Includes current bar i\n    result = self.strategy.generate_signal(window)\n```\n**Issue:** Strategy sees bar `i`'s full OHLC before generating signal for bar `i`. This is look-ahead bias. Signal should be generated on `data.iloc[:i]` (bars0 to i-1) and executed at bar `i` open.\n\n### MEDIUM: Optimization Selects on Training Data Only, But No Statistical Correction\n**File:** `engine/optimization/grid_search.py:26-33`\n```python\nranked = sorted(results, key=lambda result: (\n    result.report.equity.total_return_pct,  # Single metric selection\n    result.report.trade.profit_factor,\n    result.report.trade.win_rate_pct,\n), reverse=True)\n```\n**Issue:** Selects best parameters by total return only - no multiple testing correction (Bonferroni, Benjamini-Hochberg, or deflated Sharpe).
